## What Software & Hardware I Found

With some researching, I found some software that can be used to educate people on nuclear reactors. I originally wanted to look at software people would use daily at their job in this industry, but those were too restricted to have much detail on them, so I chose software that were for education instead. For hardware, since I'm also trying to pitch the safety of nuclear power to people, I chose to include details about protection systems used in a nuclear reactor. When I was searching for software, I first searched "nuclear reactor simulation", and many variations of that search to find a page with sufficient detail on training software, but I eventually opted for "nuclear reactor 3D model" and found most of what I had listed. For hardware, I knew it from the top of my head, so I searched "reactor protection system" and got two Wikipedia articles about it.

## Sources

[Energy Encyclopedia](https://www.energyencyclopedia.com/en/online-3d-models) was a great place for some educational software related to my topic. It contained 3D models in good detail of the two most common nuclear reactor types in the industry, the [Pressurized Water Reactor](https://3d.energyencyclopedia.com/npp-pwr/) and the [Boiling Water Reactor](https://3d.energyencyclopedia.com/npp-bwr). Theres also way more about future systems that I could use later. [This simulation](https://dalton-nrs.manchester.ac.uk/) by the University of Manchester is also great. There isn't much to operate, but its the basics. For hardware, Wikipedia had the most straight-forward [articles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boiling_water_reactor_safety_systems#Reactor_Protection_System_(RPS)), while [this source](https://nrcoe.inl.gov/publicdocs/SystemStudies/rps-bw-description.pdf) was more detailed, but I had to dig through it to find specific information.

## Engineering Design Process (EDP)

Currently, I am on the second step of the Engineering Design Process, or EDP, where I research the problem. Because the problem is that some people are not informed of the safety of nuclear power and the effectiveness of nuclear power, I looked for software that they could look at in order to get a grasp of how nuclear power plants work. Then I looked for hardware that protects said power plants so people can understand their safety. The next step of the EDP will be to brainstorm solutions, and in that case, I'll have to think of how to put together a website to inform people about nuclear power plants.

## Skills

Two skills I've picked up during the research is **how to read documentation** and **how to Google.**

#### How to read documentation

One of the sources for hardware was not a normal informative article. It was a [system description document](https://nrcoe.inl.gov/publicdocs/SystemStudies/rps-bw-description.pdf) describing the functions of the RPS and used unfamiliar terms. I learned to better sort through the intimidatingly long passages with unfamiliar terms by skimming through pages for terms I knew until I reached a page which had what I needed and looked through the titles.

#### How to Google

I better improved my knowledge on searching by varying my terms as they could get different results. For example, instead of going straight for putting the word "software" in my search, I tried terms such as "simulations" or "models" which brought me better educational software rather than professional software I couldn't actually use or get sufficient information for.

## Next Steps

The next EDP step is brainstorming how to better inform people of my topic/industry, and I have decent hopes for that.
