## What Software & Hardware I Found

With some researching, I found some software that can be used to educate people on nuclear reactors. I originally wanted to look at software people would use daily at their job in this industry, but those were too restricted to have much detail on them, so I chose software that were for education instead. For hardware, since I'm also trying to pitch the safety of nuclear power to people, I chose to include details about protection systems used in a nuclear reactor. When I was searching for software, I first searched "nuclear reactor simulation", and many variations of that search to find a page with sufficient detail on training software, but I eventually opted for "nuclear reactor 3D model" and found most of what I had listed. For hardware, I knew it from the top of my head, so I searched "reactor protection system" and got two Wikipedia articles about it.
